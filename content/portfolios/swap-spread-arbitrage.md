When swap rates and Treasury yields of comparable maturity diverge, this portfolio investigates whether a hedged position can profit as the relationship normalizes. Exchange-traded swap and Treasury futures provide a practical way to implement and test the strategy.

Analysts refine signals, backtest trading rules and develop risk controls, including DV01 hedging and volatility targeting, while assessing transaction costs and execution.
