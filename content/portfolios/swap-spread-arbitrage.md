When the spread between an interest-rate swap and a Treasury bond of the same maturity becomes unusually large, a relative-value position can seek to profit as the spread converges.

The team tests risk management techniques, including DV01 hedging and volatility targeting, to maximize risk-adjusted returns.

Analysts will lead R&D work that extends this strategy through backtesting, machine-learning methods, and practical implementation. Analysts gain a working knowledge of derivatives and interest-rate markets.
