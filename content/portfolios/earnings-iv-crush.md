Options often become expensive before earnings announcements as investors pay for protection against unexpected news. This portfolio studies whether selling that uncertainty can capture the subsequent decline in implied volatility.

Summer research found that the original term-structure filter selected larger risks rather than better opportunities. Analysts now investigate mispricing signals, event selection and execution costs through historical testing and forward monitoring.
